飯飯兒童安全標準

飯飯 fanfan 是只給 18 歲以上成年人使用的吃飯約會服務。我們對兒少性虐待與性剝削（CSAE）及兒少性虐待內容（CSAM）採取零容忍。

只限成年人

  • 註冊時必須填寫生日，未滿 18 歲無法建立帳號。
  • 只要有具體證據顯示使用者未滿 18 歲（例如本人自述、提到國高中年級、制服照或照片明顯是未成年人），帳號會被停權。
  • 被停權的裝置與手機號碼不能重新註冊。

禁止的行為

  • 任何涉及未成年人的性內容、性暗示、裸露影像，或以未成年人為對象的邀約、誘騙、性交易或金錢交換。
  • 使用者違反以上任何一項，帳號會立即停權，不另行警告。

我們如何防範

  • 公開欄位與聊天訊息會自動過濾性交易、包養、未成年相關字詞；疑似違規的訊息會先被扣住，由審核人員確認。
  • 聊天只在雙方都確認赴約之後才開放，見面地點是公開的餐廳。
  • 真人驗證以自拍比對個人照片，自拍在審核後 7 天內刪除。

如何檢舉

  • 在 App 裡打開對方的個人檔案或聊天室，點右上角選單的「檢舉」，原因選「疑似未成年」。
  • 也可以寫信到 tw466376@gmail.com，附上對方的名字與你看到的情況。
  • 每一則新檢舉都會即時通知審核人員；疑似未成年的檢舉最優先，12 小時內處理。

處理與通報

  • 確認有兒少性剝削或兒少性影像時，我們當天停權相關帳號、讓內容無法再被看到、保存必要紀錄，並依《兒童及少年性剝削防制條例》等台灣法令通報主管機關與警方（110、113 保護專線），網路內容並通報 iWIN 網路內容防護機構。
  • 我們配合執法機關依法調閱資料的請求。

聯絡窗口

兒童安全相關問題與通報：tw466376@gmail.com。遇到緊急狀況請直接撥打 110；兒少保護請撥 113 保護專線（24 小時）。

fanfan Child Safety Standards

fanfan (飯飯) is a dinner-dating service for adults 18 and over only. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Adults only

  • A birthdate is required at sign-up; nobody under 18 can create an account.
  • Any concrete evidence that a member is under 18 (their own statement, a mention of a school grade, a school uniform photo, photos clearly of a minor) gets the account banned.
  • Banned devices and phone numbers cannot sign up again.

What is prohibited

  • Any sexual content, sexualised remarks or nude images involving minors, and any solicitation, grooming, sexual transaction or exchange of money aimed at a minor.
  • Any of these gets the account banned immediately, without a warning.

How we prevent it

  • Public fields and chats are filtered for words about sex work, sugar dating and minors; suspected violations are held for a moderator to review.
  • Chat opens only after both people confirm a seat, and meetings take place in public restaurants.
  • Real-person verification compares a selfie with the profile photos; the selfie is deleted within 7 days of review.

How to report

  • In the app, open the person's profile or the chat, tap the menu at the top right, choose Report and the reason "May be under 18".
  • Or email tw466376@gmail.com with the person's name and what you saw.
  • Moderators are notified of every new report right away; reports about possible minors come first and are handled within 12 hours.

Action and reporting to authorities

  • When CSAE or CSAM is confirmed, we ban the accounts involved the same day, make the content unavailable, keep the records required, and report to the competent authorities and police under Taiwan law (including the Child and Youth Sexual Exploitation Prevention Act), and to iWIN for online content.
  • We cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement.

Contact

Child safety questions and reports: tw466376@gmail.com. In an emergency in Taiwan, call 110; for child protection, call the 113 hotline (24 hours).
