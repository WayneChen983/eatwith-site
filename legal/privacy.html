隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 26 日

這份政策說明飯約（DinnerDate）蒐集哪些資料、如何使用，以及你的權利。

1. 我們蒐集的資料

  • 帳號：iPhone 上是 Apple 登入識別碼，以及 Apple 提供的 email（可能是轉寄地址）；Android 上是 Google 登入提供的帳號識別碼、名稱與 email；如果你驗證手機，還有手機號碼。
  • 個人檔案：暱稱、生日（其他人只看得到年齡）、性別（可不填）、職業、自介、興趣、照片、所在區域與飯局偏好。
  • 活動：你發起與申請的飯局、配對、出席與回報、聊天訊息、檢舉與封鎖。
  • 真人驗證自拍：只用來審核，審核後 7 天內刪除。
  • 報到位置：只有在你按「我到了」時讀取一次位置，而且只儲存你與所選區域中心的距離，不儲存座標。
  • 裝置：推播通知 token；安裝識別碼（iPhone 上是存在鑰匙圈的隨機識別碼，Android 上是由 Android ID 衍生、只屬於本 App 與你裝置的識別碼）；以及 iPhone 上的 Apple DeviceCheck 狀態。安裝識別碼與 DeviceCheck 只用來阻擋被停權的裝置。
  • 購買：App Store 與 Google Play 的方案與加購交易資料。我們不會取得你的信用卡資訊。

2. 我們如何使用

提供與改善本服務、排序你看到的飯局、處理檢舉與防止詐騙及濫用（包括自動偵測異常與裝置封鎖）、傳送通知，以及回覆你的詢問。

3. 我們不做的事

我們不販售你的資料、不放廣告、不跨 App 追蹤你，也不使用第三方分析工具。

4. 誰看得到什麼

  • 其他會員看得到你的個人檔案與可靠度，但看不到你的生日、手機號碼或確切位置。
  • 餐廳名稱依主揪的設定，只顯示給同桌的人，或公開給所有人。
  • 聊天內容只有同一桌的成員看得到。

5. 服務供應商

  • Supabase：資料庫與檔案儲存，位於 AWS 日本東京區域。
  • Apple（iPhone）：登入、推播通知、付款與 DeviceCheck。
  • Google（Android）：登入、推播通知（Firebase Cloud Messaging）、付款（Google Play），以及在手機上執行的照片與自拍人臉偵測（ML Kit）；這些程式庫可能會傳送匿名的效能診斷資料給 Google。
  • 若啟用手機驗證，會透過簡訊服務供應商傳送驗證碼。

6. 保存期間

  • 聊天訊息在 180 天後刪除；被檢舉或標記的訊息會保留到審核完成。
  • 驗證自拍在審核後 7 天內刪除。
  • 刪除帳號時，你的個人檔案、照片、你主揪的飯局與你的申請會被刪除；你在別人飯局聊天室傳的訊息會以匿名留在該聊天室，隨聊天室在 180 天後刪除。為了安全所需的紀錄（例如你提出的檢舉、停權與裝置封鎖）會保留。

7. 你的權利

依個人資料保護法，你可以查詢、閱覽、要求複本、補充或更正、停止使用或刪除你的資料。你可以在 App 內編輯個人檔案，或在「個人 › 帳號 › 刪除帳號」刪除帳號；其他請求請來信。

8. 未成年人

本服務僅供 18 歲以上使用，我們不會蓄意蒐集未成年人的資料。

9. 安全

資料在傳輸時加密，資料庫以列級權限控管，只有你與必要的審核人員能存取你的私人資料。

10. 政策變更

我們可能更新本政策，重大變更會在 App 內通知。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy explains what DinnerDate (飯約) collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. What we collect

  • Account: on iPhone, your Sign in with Apple identifier and the email Apple shares (possibly a relay address); on Android, your Google account identifier, name and email from Sign in with Google; your phone number if you verify it.
  • Profile: name, birthday (others only see your age), gender (optional), occupation, bio, interests, photos, area and meal preferences.
  • Activity: meals you post and ask to join, matches, attendance and outcomes, chat messages, reports and blocks.
  • Verification selfie: used only for review and deleted within 7 days after it.
  • Check-in location: read once, only when you tap "I'm here", and stored only as your distance to the chosen area's centre, never as coordinates.
  • Device: push notification token; an install id (on iPhone a random id kept in the keychain, on Android one derived from the Android ID, which is unique to this app on your device); and on iPhone, Apple DeviceCheck state. The install id and DeviceCheck are used only to block suspended devices.
  • Purchases: App Store and Google Play transactions for plans and packs. We never see your card details.

2. How we use it

To run and improve the service, rank the meals you see, handle reports and prevent scams and abuse (including automatic anomaly detection and device blocks), send notifications, and answer your questions.

3. What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, track you across apps, or use third-party analytics.

4. Who sees what

  • Other members see your profile and reliability, but not your birthday, phone number or exact location.
  • A restaurant's name is shown only to the people with a seat at the table, or to everyone, as the host chooses.
  • Chats are visible only to the people at that table.

5. Service providers

  • Supabase: database and file storage, in the AWS Tokyo (Japan) region.
  • Apple (iPhone): sign-in, push notifications, payments and DeviceCheck.
  • Google (Android): sign-in, push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), payments (Google Play), and the face check on photos and selfies, which runs on your phone (ML Kit). These libraries may send Google anonymous performance diagnostics.
  • If phone verification is turned on, an SMS provider sends the codes.

6. How long we keep it

  • Chat messages are deleted after 180 days; reported or flagged messages are kept until reviewed.
  • Verification selfies are deleted within 7 days after review.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, photos, the meals you host and your requests are deleted. Messages you sent at other people's tables stay in that chat, without your name, until the chat is deleted (after 180 days). Records needed for safety (such as reports you made, suspensions and device blocks) are kept.

7. Your rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act you can ask to see, get a copy of, correct, stop the use of, or delete your data. You can edit your profile in the app and delete your account in Profile › Account › Delete account; for anything else, email us.

8. Minors

The service is only for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and row-level security in the database limits your private data to you and the moderators who need it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will tell you in the app about significant changes.
