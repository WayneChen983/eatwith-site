1. 我們蒐集的資料

  • 帳號：iPhone 上是 Apple 登入識別碼，以及 Apple 提供的 email（可能是轉寄地址）；Android 上是 Google 登入提供的帳號識別碼、名稱與 email；如果你驗證手機，還有手機號碼。

  • 關於我（都可以不填）：身高、飲酒、抽菸、運動、學歷、語言、星座、寵物、小孩。
  • 探索設定：想認識的人（女生、男生或所有人）、距離與年齡範圍、是否隱藏年齡、是否暫停讓別人看到你的飯局。
  • 活動：你發起與申請的飯局、申請時附的一句話、配對、出席與飯後回報、聊天訊息與已讀時間、你送出的「想跟你吃飯」與附言、檢舉與封鎖。
  • 使用紀錄：你哪幾天開啟 App（只記日期與 iPhone／Android，不記你在 App 裡做了什麼），以及最後一次使用的時間。
  • 真人驗證自拍：只用來審核，審核後 7 天內刪除。

  • 裝置：推播通知 token、App 版本、系統語言；安裝識別碼（iPhone 上是存在鑰匙圈的隨機識別碼，Android 上是由 Android ID 衍生、只屬於本 App 與你裝置的識別碼）；以及 iPhone 上的 Apple DeviceCheck 狀態。安裝識別碼與 DeviceCheck 只用來阻擋被停權的裝置。
  • 購買：App Store 與 Google Play 的方案、試用與加購交易資料。我們不會取得你的信用卡資訊。
  • 伺服器紀錄：你連線時，我們的服務供應商 Supabase 會自動記錄 IP 位址、時間與請求的網址，用於安全與除錯。

2. 我們如何使用

  • 提供本服務：建立帳號、顯示你的個人檔案與飯局、媒合、聊天、報到與可靠度紀錄。
  • 排序與推薦：依你的偏好、所在城市與距離排序飯局，挑選「今日精選」。
  • 安全與防止濫用：自動偵測聊天、附言與公開欄位（自介、飯局說明等）裡的違規字詞與聯絡方式，高風險的訊息會先隱藏；處理檢舉、偵測異常（例如反覆放鳥）、阻擋被停權的裝置與手機號碼。被檢舉或被標記的內容由審核人員查看。
  • 付款：驗證購買、開通方案與加購、處理退款。
  • 通知：申請、配對、聊天、飯前提醒與飯後詢問。只有你在通知設定裡開啟每週五的週末飯局推薦，我們才會在週五告訴你這個週末有哪些飯局；你可以隨時關閉。
  • 統計：用使用日期與飯局資料計算整體的使用情形（例如留存率、成局率），以改善服務。統計結果不會指出任何個人。
  • 回覆你的詢問與個資請求。

依個人資料保護法，以上屬於下列特定目的：契約、類似契約或其他法律關係事務（069）、消費者、客戶管理與服務（090）、網路購物及其他電子商務服務（148）、資（通）訊服務（135）、資（通）訊與資料庫管理（136）、資通安全與管理（137）、調查、統計與研究分析（157），以及你同意時的行銷（040，只有每週五的週末飯局推薦）。

3. 位置

  • 瀏覽「探索」與「今日精選」時，App 會把你當下的大致位置（約百公尺精度）傳到伺服器，用來決定你在哪個城市、計算你和飯局的距離。伺服器不儲存你的座標；今日精選只會記下當天你所在的城市，兩天內刪除。
  • 在飯局按「我到了」時，App 會讀一次位置，只儲存你和飯局區域中心的距離，不儲存座標。
  • App 不會在背景追蹤你的位置，其他會員也看不到你的位置，只看得到飯局所在的區域與大約的距離。
  • 你可以在手機的系統設定關閉定位權限。關閉後，探索改用你個人檔案裡的區域；「我到了」需要定位才能使用。

4. 我們不做的事

我們不販售你的資料、不放廣告、不跨 App 追蹤你，也不使用第三方分析或廣告工具。

5. 誰看得到什麼

  • 其他會員看得到你的暱稱、年齡（Plus 以上可以隱藏）、性別、職業、自介、興趣、照片、關於我、真人驗證徽章、付費會員徽章（不顯示是哪一種方案）與可靠度紀錄；看不到你的生日、手機號碼、email、確切位置或「想認識的人」設定。
  • 想跟你吃飯：你送出後，對方會收到「有人想跟你吃飯」。Gold 與 Platinum 會員看得到是誰送的；如果你們互相都有，雙方都會知道。Platinum 會員送出時可以附一句話，對方即使還不知道是誰也看得到這句話。「想跟你吃飯」14 天後失效。
  • 聊天：只有同一桌的成員看得到。同桌的 Platinum 會員可以看到大家讀到哪裡（已讀時間）。
  • 餐廳名稱：依主揪的設定，只顯示給同桌的人，或公開給所有人。
  • 審核人員：為了處理檢舉與維護安全，審核人員可以查看帳號資料、被檢舉或被標記的內容，以及相關的聊天紀錄。

6. 服務供應商與資料所在地

我們只把資料交給替我們處理的服務供應商，不提供給其他第三方，除非法律要求（例如檢警依法調閱）。

  • Supabase：資料庫、檔案儲存與伺服器紀錄，資料存放在 AWS 日本東京區域。
  • Apple（iPhone）：登入、推播通知、付款與 DeviceCheck。
  • Google（Android）：登入、推播通知（Firebase Cloud Messaging）、付款（Google Play），以及在手機上執行的照片與自拍人臉偵測（ML Kit）；這些程式庫可能會傳送匿名的效能診斷資料給 Google。iPhone 上的人臉偵測同樣只在手機上執行。
  • 若啟用手機驗證，會透過簡訊服務供應商傳送驗證碼。

Apple 與 Google 的推播與付款服務可能在台灣以外（例如美國）處理資料。

7. 保存期間

  • 帳號與個人檔案：保存到你刪除帳號。
  • 聊天訊息：180 天後刪除；被檢舉或被標記的訊息會保留到處理完成後 1 年。
  • 驗證自拍：審核後 7 天內刪除。
  • 伺服器紀錄：由 Supabase 短期保存（最多約 7 天）。
  • 刪除帳號時，你的個人檔案、照片、關於我、你主揪的飯局、你的申請、「想跟你吃飯」、推播 token 與安裝紀錄會被刪除；你在別人飯局聊天室傳的訊息會以匿名留在該聊天室，隨聊天室在 180 天後刪除。

刪除帳號後仍會保留的資料：

  • 交易紀錄：與你的帳號斷開後保留，用於退款、帳務與稅務。
  • 使用日期：改成無法連回你的匿名紀錄，用於整體統計。
  • 為了安全保留的紀錄：你提出的檢舉、停權與裝置、手機號碼封鎖紀錄；以及別人對你的檢舉與系統標記，會與帳號斷開、只留必要摘要（暱稱、年齡、性別、城市、註冊時間，以及手機號碼的雜湊值與安裝識別碼），保留 180 天後刪除，供審核與配合司法調查。如果刪除時你正被檢舉或審核，你的裝置與手機號碼之後無法再用來註冊。

8. 你的權利

依個人資料保護法，你可以：查詢或請求閱覽、請求製給複製本、請求補充或更正、請求停止蒐集、處理或利用，以及請求刪除你的資料。

  • 在 App 裡：「個人」可以編輯個人檔案、關於我與通知；「個人 › 帳號 › 刪除帳號」可以立即刪除帳號。
  • 其他請求請用你登入時的 email 寫信給我們。我們會在 15 天內回覆查詢、閱覽與複製本的請求，30 天內回覆補充、更正、停止利用與刪除的請求；需要延長時會先告訴你原因，最多延長一次。
  • 撤回同意：在手機的系統設定關閉定位或通知權限，或在 App 的通知設定關閉個別類別（包括每週五的週末飯局推薦）。

你可以不提供某些資料，但會影響部分功能：不提供生日就無法使用（本服務只限 18 歲以上）；不給定位，探索會改用你個人檔案的區域，也無法按「我到了」；不開通知就收不到申請與飯前提醒。

1. What we collect

2. How we use it

  • To run the service: your account, showing your profile and meals, matching, chat, check-in and your reliability record.
  • To rank and recommend: sorting meals by your preferences, the city you're in and distance, and choosing Top Picks.
  • For safety and to prevent abuse: automatically detecting banned words and contact details in chats, notes and public fields (your bio, a meal's note and so on), holding back high-risk messages; handling reports, detecting anomalies (such as repeated no-shows), and blocking suspended devices and phone numbers. Moderators review reported or flagged content.
  • For payments: verifying purchases, turning on plans and packs, and handling refunds.
  • For notifications: requests, matches, chat, reminders before a meal and the question afterwards. We send the Friday round-up of the weekend's meals only if you turn it on in notification settings, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • For statistics: using the days of use and meal data to measure the service as a whole (such as retention and how many meals fill), to improve it. The results never identify anyone.
  • To answer your questions and data requests.

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act these are the specific purposes of contract and similar legal relationships (069), consumer and customer management and services (090), online shopping and other e-commerce services (148), information and communication services (135), information and database management (136), information security and management (137), surveys, statistics and research analysis (157), and, if you agree, marketing (040, only the Friday round-up of the weekend's meals).

3. Location

  • When you browse Explore and Top Picks, the app sends your approximate location (to about 100 metres) to our server to work out which city you're in and how far each meal is. The server does not store your coordinates; Top Picks keep only the city you were in that day, deleted within two days.
  • When you tap "I'm here" at a meal, the app reads your location once and stores only your distance to the meal's area centre, never the coordinates.
  • The app never tracks your location in the background, and other members never see your location: only the meal's area and roughly how far it is.
  • You can turn off location access in your phone's settings. Explore then uses the area in your profile; "I'm here" needs location to work.

4. What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, track you across apps, or use third-party analytics or advertising tools.

5. Who sees what

  • Other members see your name, age (Plus and above can hide it), gender, occupation, bio, interests, photos, about you, the verified-person badge, the paid-member badge (not which plan) and your reliability record. They never see your birthday, phone number, email, exact location or who you'd like to meet.
  • Want to have dinner: when you send one, they hear that someone wants to have dinner with them. Gold and Platinum members can see who sent it; if it's mutual, you both find out. Platinum members can add a line, which the other person can read even before they know who sent it. A "want to have dinner" expires after 14 days.
  • Chat: only the people at that table see it. Platinum members at the table can see how far everyone has read (read times).
  • Restaurant name: shown only to the people with a seat at the table, or to everyone, as the host chooses.
  • Moderators: to handle reports and keep people safe, moderators can see account details, reported or flagged content and the chats around it.

6. Service providers and where data is kept

We share data only with the providers that process it for us, and with no one else unless the law requires it (for example, a lawful request from the police or a court).

  • Supabase: database, file storage and server logs, in the AWS Tokyo (Japan) region.
  • Apple (iPhone): sign-in, push notifications, payments and DeviceCheck.
  • Google (Android): sign-in, push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), payments (Google Play), and the face check on photos and selfies, which runs on your phone (ML Kit). These libraries may send Google anonymous performance diagnostics. On iPhone the face check also runs only on your phone.
  • If phone verification is turned on, an SMS provider sends the codes.

Apple's and Google's push and payment services may process data outside Taiwan (for example in the United States).

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Chat messages: deleted after 180 days; reported or flagged messages are kept until a year after the review is finished.
  • Verification selfies: deleted within 7 days after review.
  • Server logs: kept briefly by Supabase (up to about 7 days).
  • When you delete your account, your profile, photos, about you, the meals you host, your requests, your "want to have dinner", push tokens and install records are deleted. Messages you sent at other people's tables stay in that chat without your name until the chat is deleted (after 180 days).

What is kept after you delete your account:

  • Transactions: kept without a link to your account, for refunds, accounting and tax.
  • Days of use: turned into an anonymous record that can't be linked back to you, for overall statistics.
  • Records kept for safety: reports you made, suspensions and device and phone number blocks; and reports and automatic flags about you, unlinked from the account with only the necessary summary (name, age, gender, city, when you joined, and a hash of your phone number and your install ids), deleted after 180 days; they are kept for review and to assist lawful investigations. If you were being reported or reviewed when you deleted your account, your device and phone number can't be used to sign up again.

8. Your rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act you can ask to see your data, get a copy, add to or correct it, stop its collection, processing or use, or have it deleted.

  • In the app: Profile lets you edit your profile, about you and notifications; Profile › Account › Delete account deletes your account right away.
  • For anything else, email us from the email you signed in with. We reply within 15 days to requests to see or copy your data, and within 30 days to requests to add to, correct, stop using or delete it; if we need longer we'll tell you why first, and extend only once.
  • To withdraw consent: turn off location or notification access in your phone's settings, or turn off individual kinds of notification (including the Friday round-up) in the app.

You can choose not to give some data, but parts of the service then won't work: without a birthday you can't use it (it's only for people 18 and older); without location, Explore uses the area in your profile and you can't tap "I'm here"; without notifications you won't hear about requests or reminders.

9. Minors

The service is only for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from minors, and we suspend any account we find belongs to one.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and row-level security in the database limits your private data to you and the moderators who need it. If a data breach may affect you, we will tell you in the app or by email once we've established what happened.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We'll tell you in the app about significant changes and update the date above.
