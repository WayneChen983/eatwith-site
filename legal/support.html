常見問題

怎麼提高被選上的機會？

放幾張清楚的本人照片、寫一段自介、填「關於我」，並完成真人驗證。申請時附一句話，說說你為什麼想一起吃這頓飯。準時赴約、不放鳥，可靠度高的人會排得比較前面。

聊天室什麼時候開？

主揪接受你的申請、你按下「確認赴約」之後，聊天室就會開啟。主揪接受後請在期限內確認，逾時座位會釋出給別人。

對方放鳥，或回報和我不一樣怎麼辦？

飯局開始後 48 小時內，雙方都可以回報有沒有見到面。說法不一樣時會記為「爭議」，不會扣任何一方的可靠度；同一個人反覆出現爭議或放鳥，會由審核人員處理。覺得對方有問題，也可以直接檢舉。

怎麼取消訂閱？

iPhone：「設定 › Apple ID › 訂閱項目」，請在當期結束前至少 24 小時取消。Android：「Google Play › 付款和訂閱 › 訂閱」，請在續訂日前取消。取消後，方案會持續到已付費的期間結束。刪除帳號不會自動取消訂閱。

怎麼退費？

iPhone 的購買由 Apple 處理，我們無法代為退款：到 reportaproblem.apple.com 登入你的 Apple ID 申請。Android 的購買：在 Google Play 的訂單記錄找到那筆購買申請退款，或寫信給我們。退款後，那筆購買增加的方案或次數會被收回。

Gold 免費試用會自動扣款嗎？

會。7 天試用結束後會自動轉為 Gold 月方案並開始收費。不想付費，iPhone 請在試用結束前至少 24 小時取消，Android 請在試用結束前取消（方法同上）。

真人驗證沒通過

App 會告訴你原因（例如照片不清楚、手勢不符，或和個人照片看起來不是同一個人）。在光線充足的地方，照 App 指定的手勢重拍一次就可以重新送出。

換手機（iPhone ↔ Android）

同一個平台換新手機：用同一個 Apple ID 或 Google 帳號登入即可，資料都在。iPhone 換 Android（或反過來）：iPhone 用 Apple 登入、Android 用 Google 登入，目前帳號無法跨平台，在新平台登入會是新帳號。付費方案跟著 Apple 或 Google 的商店帳號，不會轉到另一個平台，請在原平台取消。需要把赴約紀錄移到新帳號，請寫信給我們並附上兩邊的帳號 ID，我們會個別處理。

Questions

How do I get picked more often?

Add a few clear photos of yourself, write a bio, fill in "about you" and get the verified-person badge. Send a line with your request saying why you'd like to join that meal. Showing up on time and never standing anyone up keeps your reliability high, and reliable people are shown first.

When does the chat open?

Once the host accepts your request and you tap "Confirm my seat". Confirm before the deadline after you're accepted, or the seat goes to someone else.

They didn't show up, or said something different from me

For 48 hours after a meal starts, both of you can say whether you met. If you disagree it's recorded as a dispute and nobody's reliability goes down; moderators look at anyone with repeated disputes or no-shows. If something was wrong, you can also report them.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iPhone: Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends. Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, before the renewal date. The plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases on iPhone are handled by Apple, and we can't refund them for you: go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID. Purchases on Android: find the purchase in your Google Play order history and ask for a refund there, or email us. After a refund, what the purchase added is removed.

Does the free Gold trial turn into a paid plan?

Yes. After 7 days it becomes Gold Monthly and you're charged. If you don't want to pay, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends on iPhone, or before it ends on Android (as above).

My verification wasn't approved

The app tells you why (for example the photo was blurry, the gesture didn't match, or it didn't look like the person in your photos). Retake it in good light with the gesture the app asks for and send it again.

I changed my phone number

Email us from the email you signed in with, with your account ID and the new number, and we'll sort it out with you. One phone number can only belong to one account.

My account was suspended. How do I appeal?

Tap "Contact support" on the suspension screen and the email includes your account ID; or email tw466376@gmail.com with the subject "Account appeal", your account ID and what happened. We'll confirm we've received it within 72 hours and tell you the outcome once we've reviewed it.

Switching phones (iPhone ↔ Android)

A new phone on the same platform: sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account and everything is there. From iPhone to Android (or back): iPhone uses Sign in with Apple and Android uses Sign in with Google, and accounts can't move between them yet, so signing in on the other platform starts a new account. A paid plan belongs to your Apple or Google store account and doesn't move to the other platform: cancel it on the old one. To move your meal record to the new account, email us with both account IDs and we'll look at it case by case.

How long is my data kept?

See section 7 of the Privacy Policy. Chat messages are deleted after 180 days, verification selfies within 7 days after review.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile › Account › Delete account. It takes effect right away.

Without the app: email tw466376@gmail.com from the email of the account you signed in with (your Apple or Google account's email), with the subject "Delete my account". If you used Apple's "Hide My Email", write from that relay address, or include the account ID from the app or your verified phone number. Once we've confirmed it's you, we delete it within 30 days and reply to let you know.

What's deleted: your profile, photos, about you, the meals you host, your requests, your "want to have dinner" and your push tokens; unused pack credits are deleted too and can't be restored. Messages you sent at other people's tables stay in that chat without your name until the chat is deleted (after 180 days). What's kept: transactions (without a link to your account, for refunds and accounting), anonymous statistics of the days the app was used, and the reports, suspensions and device blocks kept for safety (see the Privacy Policy). Cancel a paid plan separately in the App Store or Google Play; deleting your account doesn't cancel it.
