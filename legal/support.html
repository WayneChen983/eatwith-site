刪除帳號

在 App 裡：個人 › 帳號 › 刪除帳號，立即生效。

沒有 App 也可以：用你登入時的 email（Apple 或 Google 帳號的 email）寫信到 tw466376@gmail.com，主旨「刪除帳號」。我們確認是你本人後，會在 30 天內刪除並回信通知。

刪除的內容：個人檔案、照片、你主揪的飯局、你的申請與推播 token。你在別人飯局聊天室傳的訊息會以匿名留在該聊天室，隨聊天室在 180 天後刪除。為了安全保留的紀錄：你提出的檢舉、停權與裝置封鎖紀錄。付費方案請另外在 App Store 或 Google Play 取消，刪除帳號不會自動取消訂閱。

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile › Account › Delete account. It takes effect right away.

Without the app: email tw466376@gmail.com from the email of the account you signed in with (your Apple or Google account's email), with the subject "Delete my account". Once we've confirmed it's you, we delete it within 30 days and reply to let you know.

What's deleted: your profile, photos, the meals you host, your requests and your push tokens. Messages you sent at other people's tables stay in that chat without your name until the chat is deleted (after 180 days). What's kept for safety: reports you made, suspensions and device blocks. Cancel a paid plan separately in the App Store or Google Play; deleting your account doesn't cancel it.
